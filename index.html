01What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is software that understands requests and completes tasks on its own: answering customers, qualifying leads, booking meetings or updating your systems, with a person in the loop when needed.

02What is the difference between a chatbot and an AI agent?

A chatbot answers questions in a conversation. An AI agent goes further: it takes actions across your tools, such as creating a record in your CRM or scheduling a call.

03Who do you work with?

Small and mid-sized businesses and professionals who want to save time with AI, in English or Spanish.

04Do you work remotely?

Yes. We work online with clients in the United States, Latin America and Spain.

05How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. A chatbot or a first automation can be ready in a few weeks, and every proposal includes a clear timeline.
